For university students

Bulong

Say what you can’t say out loud.

No account, no name, just your message.

How it works

Pick your school, write your message, and send it anonymously.

Read what students at your school wrote, or the All schools board.

Send a hug to show someone you read it and understood.

Content warnings

Heavy messages are blurred when the writer adds a warning. Tap it to choose whether to read.

Writing something upsetting? Tick a warning before you send.

The 18+ button is a promise, not an ID check. If you’re under 18, please skip those messages.

Rules

Don’t name or describe real people, like classmates, teachers, or staff.

No phone numbers, links, addresses, or social media handles.

No bullying, hate, threats, or sexual content. Anything involving minors is never allowed.

Keep details vague. Campuses are small, so your section or schedule could give you away.

See something wrong? Press Report.

If you need help

Bulong isn’t counseling, and no one here can reply to you privately.
